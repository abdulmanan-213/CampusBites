How to Order

Getting your favorite meal at Campus Bites is easy! Follow these simple steps to place your order.

4 Easy Steps to Order

Step 1: Choose Your Food

Explore our food menu and select your favorite items. You can also check our daily deals to find affordable meal combinations.

Step 2: Place Your Order

Visit the cafeteria counter and tell the staff which food items you would like to order. Confirm your order before making payment.

Step 3: Pay at the Counter

Pay the total amount at the counter using an accepted payment method. Keep your receipt if one is provided.

Step 4: Collect Your Token and Meal

Collect your order token after payment. Listen for your token number and collect your meal from the designated pickup counter.

Important Ordering Guidelines

Peak Hours

The cafeteria may be busier during lunch breaks and between classes. Place your order early when possible and allow extra time for service.

Order Pickup Rules

  • Keep your token safe until you collect your meal.
  • Check your order before leaving the pickup counter.
  • Wait for your token number to be called.
  • Keep the pickup area clear for other students.
  • Contact cafeteria staff if your order is delayed.

Be Ready Before Ordering

Check the menu and prices in advance, choose your items, and keep your payment ready to help reduce waiting time.

Need Help?

If you have questions about an order, ask the cafeteria staff or visit our Feedback & Contact page.
